# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Optical Voice a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, nationality, appearance, disability, religion, or technical preference.

## Expected behaviour

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and assume good faith;
- welcome questions and constructive disagreement;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, measurements, and reproducible evidence;
- acknowledge mistakes and correct them openly;
- respect privacy and responsible-disclosure requests;
- avoid exaggerated technical or performance claims.

## Unacceptable behaviour

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks;
- sexualised language or unwanted attention;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- deliberately disruptive behaviour;
- falsifying benchmarks, test conditions, or authorship;
- removing required attribution from upstream or contributed work.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct. Repeated or serious violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Reports should be made privately to the maintainer whenever possible. Reports will be reviewed fairly and with respect for the privacy and safety of those involved.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository discussions, pull requests, issue trackers, project chat spaces, demonstrations, and public interactions where someone is representing the project.
